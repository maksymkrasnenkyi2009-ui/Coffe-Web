Coffee Facts

1. Coffee is the second most traded commodity in the world after oil.

2. Coffee was discovered by a goat herder in Ethiopia.

3. Coffee beans are actually seeds of the coffee fruit, also known as coffee cherries.

4. The world's largest cup of coffee was brewed in South Korea in 2014 and measured over 3,700 gallons.

5. Coffee can help improve cognitive function and boost energy levels.
